## Reasoning
Research question: with D_geo, top_k=512, graph_k=128, lambda=0.15, 200 original Jacobi iterations, and one shared Stage-2 graph fixed, do G1 inputs improve the final chart? B0+S2 was best on ks, mean_m and p95_m; Identity+S2 and Graph+S2 did not improve the complete pipeline. Graph+S2 was also worse than Identity+S2 on those primary endpoints, although its single largest error was slightly smaller. This is a negative result for this fixed combination, not a claim that all graph methods fail. The historical S2 hyperparameter-selection protocol is not independently verified; results are same-node transductive, seed 10.
